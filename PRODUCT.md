# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Faculty hiring committees, finance and fintech researchers, potential collaborators, seminar organizers, and students evaluating Shimin Zhang's academic research and teaching profile.

## Product Purpose

Present Shimin Zhang's research agenda, working papers, teaching experience, academic credentials, downloadable CV, and contact information. The primary success condition is that a faculty-search reader can understand her research identity quickly and find credible supporting evidence without distraction.

## Positioning

An academic profile centered on the market structure and economic incentives of blockchain-based financial markets, with particular interests in Bitcoin and cryptocurrencies, tokenization, stablecoins, and AI agents in finance.

## Operating Context

The site is read during faculty searches, research discovery, conference follow-up, and collaboration outreach. It is published at shiminzhang.com through the project's established GitHub Pages workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five primary sections: Home, Research, Teaching, CV, and Contact.
- No Publications section until publication evidence exists.
- Research assistant experience is supporting context rather than a headline credential.
- The downloadable CV is the authoritative full record and currently states Expected 2027.
- Research methods such as empirical analysis, economic theory, machine learning, and NLP are presented as approaches serving the core research questions.
- Contact actions must be honest and usable; no simulated message delivery.

## Brand Commitments

- English name "Shimin Zhang" is primary; Chinese name "张时敏" is secondary.
- "逊志时敏 · Humble in purpose · diligent in learning" appears only on Home.
- The approved identity uses a soft Monet-inspired field of pale lavender, pink, blue, yellow, and restrained jade.
- The name treatment may use only the pale center of the approved mineral texture with feathered edges; avoid cheap-looking crystal frames, fluorite edges, lotus imagery, and unrelated crypto iconography.
- Preserve the supplied portrait and the approved blockchain research artwork as atmospheric background material, not as a diagram claiming one-to-one conceptual relationships.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

- Lead with a coherent research question, not a catalogue of buzzwords.
- Separate research domains, economic questions, outcomes, and methods.
- Prioritize faculty-search-relevant evidence and omit unsupported claims.
- Keep personal character visible while maintaining academic credibility.
- Make every section legible and complete on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic headings, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
